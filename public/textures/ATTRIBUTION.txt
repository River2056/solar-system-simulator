Solar System Scope textures
===========================

Files in this directory are adapted from the Solar System Scope texture pack:
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

The source textures are based on NASA elevation and imagery data and are distributed
under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Downloaded at 2K resolution and renamed for use by this simulator.
